FatTracker - Pure mode floppy sounds
====================================
Put your own Amiga drive recordings here (WAV or IFF 8SVX, mono or stereo):

  floppy_motor.wav   - motor running (played in a loop while the drive spins)
  floppy_step.wav    - one head step click
  floppy_insert.wav  - disk being inserted

Missing files are replaced by built-in synthesized sounds.
The folder is searched next to FatTracker.exe first, then in the working folder.
