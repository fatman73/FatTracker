Module loaders
--------------

S3M, IT (incl. IT214 / IT215 sample decompression), MTM, 669, OctaMED, DigiBooster, Symphonie
  structures and effect conversion follow OpenMPT (OpenMPT Devs;        BSD 3-clause
  the Symphonie loader by Devin Acker)
  https://openmpt.org

Own code written in the style of known algorithms: the LH5 (LHA) unpacker, the deflate / gzip decoder
(after puff by Mark Adler, zlib), the ICE 2.4 depacker. The Paula / Amiga mixer, the ProTracker-style editor,
the Pure mode screen, the fonts, the demo effects and everything else are FatTracker's own code.


Not part of FatTracker
----------------------

The music in the modules folder (test tunes from the High Voltage SID Collection, Modland, the SNDH archive
and other archives) belongs to its composers. It is not distributed with FatTracker. The same goes for the
sc68 replay routines in modules/AtariST/Replay (sc68 by Benjamin Gerard, GNU GPL).
